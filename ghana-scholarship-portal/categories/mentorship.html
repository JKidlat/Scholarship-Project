1. KUFOUR SCHOLARS PROGRAM

We seek “to promote effective leadership as the catalyst for good governance, and socio-economic development through advocacy, programmatic interventions and strategic partnerships with likeminded organizations and institutions.”

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Must be a Ghanaian citizen
  • Minimum CGPA of 3.0

  • Signed application letter
  • Admission letter clearly stating the programme's duration

2. PK AMOABENG LEADERSHIP FOUNDATION

The PK Amoabeng Leadership Scholar Program is an intense one (1) year program focused on Revolutionizing the Mindset For The African Leader. The Program seeks to add value to the young leader's mind. It is expected to produce industry leaders imbued with a strong sense of patriotism, innovative leaders, problem solvers, creative thinkers, and knowledgeable in their respective fields of endeavor.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Must be a Ghanaian citizen
  • Must be between the ages of 18 and 40

Nature Of Benefits

  • Intentional Mentorship
  • Experimental Job Shadowing
  • Courses and Seminars/li>
  • Networking and Career building opportunities
  • Introductory Bootcamp

3. ASPIRE LEADERS PROGRAM

We prepare limited-income and first-generation university students worldwide to be leaders in their communities and reach their full potential academically and professionally through a fully funded leadership development program.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • 18-29 Years
  • Must be a first-generation university student/From a Limited Income Family
  • Currently enrolled in a university program or graduated from a university

Requirements for Applicants:

  • An email ID

4. YOUNG LEADERS MENTORSHIP PROGRAM

The YLMP was started in 2009 with the aim of nurturing young leaders to develop themselves and their communities. The program builds a continuous understudy system where people who have gained lessons and experience in specific fields, share their acquired knowledge with others.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Must be a 18-40 Years old
  • Ability to commit to a 1-2 year program

Requirements for Applicants:

  • Signed application letter
  • Must have a proven Professional Or Academic Background
  • Must be Fluent in English
  • Must have Strong communication Skills
